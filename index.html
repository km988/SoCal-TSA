ABOUT TSA

What is TSA?

Technology Student Association

The Technology Student Association (TSA) is a national student organization for middle and high school students interested in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics.

Members explore their interests through competitive events, hands-on projects, and leadership opportunities. Events cover areas such as engineering, computer science, design, communication, and technology research.

Our SoCal chapter

SoCal TSA is a new student-led chapter in Southern California. Our chapter brings together students interested in developing technical skills, collaborating on projects, and exploring TSA competitions.

What can members do?

  • Explore events that match their interests and experience.
  • Develop projects and practice explaining their work.
  • Build teamwork, problem-solving, and leadership skills.

To learn about chapter participation, start with our leadership team. Meeting and registration details will be added when confirmed.

PROGRAMS

TEAMS

Tests of Engineering Aptitude, Mathematics, and Science

TEAMS is an annual, theme-based competition in which students apply math, science, and engineering to real-world problems. Students collaborate to research, design, calculate, and communicate solutions.

Competition components

01

Design / Build

Use materials and an engineering brief to design and build a solution to a challenge.

02

Multiple Choice

Work together to solve questions that apply math and science to engineering situations.

03

Essay

Research the assigned topic and develop a written response submitted before competition day.

04

Mathematical Modeling

Analyze given data and develop a mathematical model to solve a problem.

Participating in TEAMS

TEAMS requires separate registration from TSA membership. Ask chapter leadership about chapter participation and consult the official website for current rules and deadlines.
